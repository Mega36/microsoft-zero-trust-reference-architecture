02 / IDENTITY & GOVERNANCE

Identity is the security perimeter.

A fictional population of employees, contractors, and guests with governed access throughout their lifecycle.

03

Identity architecture

Separate human and workload identities. Workforce and guest accounts use SSO and federation; service and application identities receive narrowly scoped permissions. Managed identities reduce credential handling for Azure workloads.

WorkforceGuestsAdministrative accountsService identitiesApplication identitiesManaged identities

Governance by design

Resource owners approve access. Entitlements have clear owners and expiry dates. Reviews focus on privileged, guest, and stale accounts, while changes are captured in an auditable lifecycle.

Owner approvalQuarterly reviewsSeparation of dutiesExpiryAudit evidence
